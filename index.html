How figures are sourced

Every number is read on the page of the agency that issues the rule — the IRS, the CFPB, the SSA, the Department of Labor. Never from a blog, a news site, another video, or an AI. Secondary sources are fine for understanding something; they are never used to establish a figure, because they copy each other and an error spreads fast.

  • 002 — Paycheck. IRS Topic no. 424 (401(k) deferrals are subject to Social Security and Medicare), IRS Topic no. 751, SSA Contribution and Benefit Base.
  • 001 — Tax brackets. IRS IR-2025-103 / Revenue Procedure 2025-32 — 2026 rate schedules and standard deduction. IRS FS-2025-10, Q7 — the 400% federal poverty line ceiling.

Figures change every year

A file named -2026 holds 2026 rules and becomes wrong on January 1st. When a year rolls over a new file is published next to the old one. The old one is never overwritten, because someone may have been using it for months.
